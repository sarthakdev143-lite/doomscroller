1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar store that stays on your device. We use both, only first-party, except for the bot check on forms.

3. Similar technologies

  • Device fingerprinting. A one-way hash of browser and device characteristics, used to prevent fraud and repeat free-credit claims. See Privacy, section 4.
  • Session replay. A recording of how pages responded to you, with form fields masked. See Privacy, section 5.
