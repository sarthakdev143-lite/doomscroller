2. What we collect

You give us

  • Waitlist: email address, and optionally what you make and a link to your channel; the referral code you arrived with.
  • Account: email, name, a password hash (never the password itself), and your Google account ID if you sign in with Google.
  • Your videos: the links and files you submit, and what we make from them: the audio, the transcript, the clips, and their titles, descriptions and hashtags.
  • Payments: handled by our payment providers. We receive the payment’s status, amount, currency, country and a reference, not your full card number.
  • Messages: anything you send to support.

Collected automatically

  • Usage: pages you view, buttons and links you click, how far you scroll, how long sections are on screen, the page that sent you and campaign tags in the link.
  • Session replay: a recording of how the page responded to you (movements, clicks, scrolling), with every form field masked. See section 5.
  • Device and network: IP address, an approximate location derived from it (country, region, city), browser and operating system, screen size, language and time zone.
  • Device fingerprint: a one-way hash of technical characteristics of your browser and device. See section 4.
  • Security records: sign-ins, failed sign-ins, rate-limit events and other signs of abuse.

4. Device fingerprinting and fraud prevention

New accounts get {{ free_credits }} free credits, once per person. To keep that true, and to stop automated sign-ups, we combine a fingerprint of your browser and device with your IP address and its network type (for example, whether it belongs to a data centre or an anonymising network).

  • The fingerprint is a hash. We store the hash, not the raw characteristics it was made from.
  • We use it to spot many accounts made from one device or network, and to decide whether free credits are granted. A match may mean an account gets no free credits or is reviewed by a person.
  • We do not use it to follow you across other websites.

5. Analytics and session replay

Our analytics and session replay run on our own servers. We set a first-party visitor cookie (vid) so visits from one browser can be counted together, and, if you later sign up, linked to your account so we can see which pages helped.

Session replay records the page’s changes as you use it, not a video of your screen. Every input field is masked before anything leaves your browser, and fields we mark as sensitive are never recorded.

Where the law requires consent, analytics and replay wait until you allow them. You can change your choice at any time with . [LAWYER REVIEW: consent model per region]
