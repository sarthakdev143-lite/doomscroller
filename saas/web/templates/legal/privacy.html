2. What we collect

On the website, today

  • Waitlist: your email address, and if you give them, what you make and a link to your channel; the referral code you arrived with, and the one we give you; the first page you opened on this visit (including any campaign tags in its link) and the site that sent you there.
  • Technical data: a one-way hash of your IP address and of its network, the approximate location (country, region, city, time zone) and network provider worked out from it, which Cloudflare data centre answered, and your browser’s user agent. We use these to stop automated and repeated sign-ups.
  • Analytics, only if you accept: pages you view, links and buttons you click, how far you scroll, the page that sent you, an approximate location from your IP address, and recordings of clicks and scrolling with every form field hidden. See section 4.

In the app, when it opens

  • Account: email, name, a password hash (never the password itself), and your Google account ID if you sign in with Google.
  • Your videos: the links and files you submit, the logos and memes you upload, and what we make from them: the audio, the transcript, the clips, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, hashtags and suggested posting times.
  • Payments: handled by our payment provider. We receive the payment’s status, amount, currency and a reference, not your card details.
  • Security records: sign-ins, failed sign-ins and other signs of abuse.

4. Analytics and recordings

We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity, and only after you press Accept on the banner. Until then neither script is loaded, and Google’s consent mode stays set to “denied”.

  • Google Analytics counts visits, pages and the buttons people press, so we know which parts of the page help.
  • Microsoft Clarity records how pages respond to clicks and scrolling, and builds heatmaps. Form fields are masked, so what you type is never recorded.
  • Both set cookies, listed on the cookie page. Your choice is stored in your browser, and you can change it at any time with .

5. Bots and abuse

Forms on the website may be protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a person, not a script, is submitting them. It looks at technical signals from your browser and does not set advertising cookies.

In the app, the free tier is once per person. To keep it that way we will combine signals such as your IP address, network type and technical characteristics of your browser and device, stored as a one-way hash. A match may mean an account gets no free tier or is reviewed by a person. We do not use these signals to follow you across other websites.{# LAWYER REVIEW: whether device signals need consent in each market #}
