1. The short version

  • A job that fails costs nothing: its credits come back automatically.
  • Unused credit packs can be refunded within [owner + LAWYER REVIEW: number] days of purchase.
  • Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and run until the end of the period you paid for.

2. Failed and cancelled jobs

Credits are reserved when a job starts. If it fails, or you cancel it before it finishes, the reserved credits return to your balance without you asking. You can see each reservation and return in your billing history.

3. Credit packs

If you have not used any of a pack’s credits, you can ask for a full refund within [number] days of buying it. Once credits from a pack have been used, the pack is not refundable. [owner decision: partial refunds of partly used packs?]

4. Subscriptions

Cancel any time from Billing. You keep the plan, and its credits, until the end of the current period, and you are not charged again. [owner + LAWYER REVIEW: refunds for a renewal you did not mean to keep, annual plans]

5. Clips you are unhappy with

If a job finished but the clips are unusable (for example, the captions are out of sync with the voice), write to us with the job ID. We look at each case and may return the credits. [owner decision]

6. Chargebacks and reversed payments

If a payment is refunded or disputed with your bank, the credits it bought are removed from your balance (never below zero) and new jobs are paused until the dispute is resolved. Please contact us first; it is faster for both of us.

Email {{ support_email }} from your account’s address with the payment reference from your billing history. Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within [number] business days.
