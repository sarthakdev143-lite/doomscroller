{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- how it works -------------------------------- #}

How it works

From a link to clips you can post.

  1. 01

    Paste a link, or upload the file.

    Links from {{ sources[:-1]|join(", ") }} and {{ sources[-1] }}, or a file up to {{ max_upload }}. Before anything runs you see the length and what it will cost.

    Only send video you made or have permission to use.

  2. 02

    It is transcribed, word by word.

    Every word gets its own timestamp. English and Hindi are captioned. When nobody is talking, the video is read for motion, loudness and scene changes instead.

  3. 03

    The moments are chosen by sentence.

    The transcript goes to the model as numbered sentences and comes back as sentence numbers, so no clip starts or stops halfway through one.

    You set how many clips (1 to {{ max_clips }}) and the longest a clip may run ({{ clip_seconds[0] }} to {{ clip_seconds[1] }} seconds).

  4. 04

    Each one is edited, not just trimmed.

    Weak sentences are dropped. Stutters, filler words and long pauses are cut. The framing changes every 1 to 2.4 seconds and stays on whoever is speaking. Emphasis gets a quick zoom, a shake and a sound.

  5. 05

    You edit in the browser, then download.

    Adjust each clip before you take it, add your own logo and memes, and rewrite the title, description and hashtags. Then download the package and post it yourself, wherever you post.

    Each package: the MP4, a thumbnail, the text, and suggested times to post.

{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- anatomy ------------------------------------- #}

What you get

Every Short comes back ready to publish, not as a rough cut.

A 1080×1920 MP4 with stereo sound for Shorts, Reels or TikTok, plus everything you need to post it. This is what is in one.

  1. 1

    Progress bar

    A thin yellow bar fills across the top as the clip plays.

  2. 2

    Hook

    A title for the first 2.5 seconds, written from what is actually said.

  3. 3

    Framing on the speaker

    Wide footage sits full width over a dimmed copy of itself. The picture moves in on whoever is talking, and only switches on a cut or a beat, so it reads as an edit.

  4. 4

    Reaction memes

    Up to three per clip, on the strongest beats. Upload your own, use the built-in set, or switch them off.

  5. 5

    Credit line

    The creator’s name stays on screen for the whole clip.

  6. 6

    Word captions

    One word lights up at a time, timed to the voice. If the source already has captions burned in, ours are left off so nothing shows twice.

  7. +

    B-roll, only where it fits

    Stock footage replaces the picture when a vision check agrees it matches the words being said. Credited in the description.

  8. +

    Sound

    Effects are synthesised, so there is nothing to license. Music is optional and ducks under the voice.

  9. +

    Your logo

    Upload it once and it goes on every clip.

Languages

Captions in English and Hindi.

Leave the language on auto, or set it per job. Hindi speech goes to a model built for Hindi and is captioned in Devanagari, in the same typeface the renderer uses.

Other languages still get clips. They are cut on motion, loudness and scene changes instead of speech, and come without word captions for now.

{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- FAQ ----------------------------------------- #}

Questions

Before you ask.

What exactly am I paying for?

Minutes of the video you send, rounded up per job. A 72-minute-40-second video counts as 73 minutes. The number of clips you take from it does not change the price. Pay as you go, or monthly.

What happens if a job fails?

Minutes are set aside when a job starts. If it fails or you cancel it, they go back to your balance.

Which links work?

{{ sources|join(", ") }}. Or upload the file directly, up to {{ max_upload }}. Private videos need to be uploaded.

Which languages are captioned?

English and Hindi (in Devanagari). Videos in other languages are still clipped, using motion, loudness and scene changes, but without word captions for now.

Can I clip someone else’s video?

Only if you have their permission. You need the rights to anything you send. Each clip carries a credit line, but a credit is not permission.

Can I edit the clips?

Yes, in the browser, before you download. Add your own logo and memes, and rewrite the title, description and hashtags. The captions follow what was said in the video.

Is there a free tier?

Yes. Your first {{ free_shorts }} Shorts or {{ free_minutes }} minutes of video are free, whichever comes first, once per person.

How long are my clips kept?

{{ retention_days }} days after they are made, then they are deleted. Download what you want to keep.

Does it post for me?

No. You get each Short as a package that is ready to publish (the MP4, a thumbnail, a title, description, hashtags and suggested times to post) and you post it yourself, on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or anywhere else. Nothing needs access to your accounts.

When can I use it?

Invites go out from the waitlist in batches. Every person who joins with your link moves you up the list.
