02 Cut

A cut can only fall between two sentences.

The transcript goes to the model as numbered sentences and comes back as sentence numbers, not timestamps. So no clip starts or stops halfway through one. You choose how many clips (1 to {{ max_clips }}) and the longest each may run ({{ clip_seconds[0] }} to {{ clip_seconds[1] }} seconds).

03 Frame

Then each one is edited, not just trimmed.

Weak sentences are dropped. Stutters, filler words and long pauses are cut. Wide footage sits full width over a dimmed copy of itself, and the picture moves in on whoever is speaking, changing every 1 to 2.4 seconds, only on a cut or a beat.

Emphasis gets a quick zoom, a shake and a sound. The effects are synthesised, so there is nothing to license; music is optional and ducks under the voice.

04 Caption

Captions keep time with the voice.

One word lights up at a time, timed to the voice. A hook title holds the first 2.5 seconds, written from what is actually said, and the creator’s name stays on screen for the whole clip.

If the source already has captions burned in, ours are left off so nothing shows twice.

  • Progress bar
  • Hook, first 2.5 s
  • Picture, on the speaker
  • Credit line
  • Word captions
