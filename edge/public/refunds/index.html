Refund policy

When payments can be refunded, and how.

1. The short version

  • A job that fails costs nothing: its minutes come back automatically.
  • Unused pay-as-you-go minutes can be refunded within [owner + LAWYER REVIEW: number] days of purchase.
  • Monthly payments can be cancelled at any time and run until the end of the month you paid for.

2. Failed and cancelled jobs

Minutes are reserved when a job starts. If it fails, or you cancel it before it finishes, the reserved minutes return to your balance without you asking. You can see each reservation and return in your billing history.

3. Pay as you go

If you have not used any of the minutes you bought, you can ask for a full refund within [number] days of buying them. Once some have been used, that purchase is not refundable. [owner decision: partial refunds of partly used purchases?]

4. Monthly

Cancel any time. You keep the month you paid for, and its minutes, until it ends, and you are not charged again. [owner + LAWYER REVIEW: refunds for a renewal you did not mean to keep; how cancelling works with the payment provider]

5. Clips you are unhappy with

If a job finished but the clips are unusable (for example, the captions are out of sync with the voice), write to us with the job ID. We look at each case and may return the minutes. [owner decision]

6. Chargebacks and reversed payments

If a payment is refunded or disputed with your bank, the minutes it bought are removed from your balance (never below zero) and new jobs are paused until the dispute is resolved. Please contact us first; it is faster for both of us.
