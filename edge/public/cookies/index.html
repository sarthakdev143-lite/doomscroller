Cookie policy

Every cookie and similar technology we use, and how to change your choice.

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · Effective: 27 November 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Local storage and session storage are similar stores that stay on your device. Analytics cookies are set only after you press Accept.
