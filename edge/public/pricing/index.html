Pricing

Pay for the minutes you send. Nothing else.

Every job is priced by the length of the video you give it, rounded up to the minute. How many Shorts you take from it, and how long they are, does not change the price.

Free to start
10 Shorts or 10 min of video, whichever comes first. Once per person.
Pay as you go
Buy minutes when you have a video to cut. For occasional posting.
Monthly
Minutes every month for people who post every week.

Prices are announced at launch.

Everyone on the waitlist hears them first.

Included

Every Short comes with all of it.

  • Cuts on sentence boundaries, never mid-sentence
  • Word-by-word captions, in English and Hindi
  • Framing that stays on whoever is speaking
  • A hook title for the opening seconds
  • Stock B-roll, only where it matches what is said
  • Sound effects, and music if you want it
  • Reaction memes: yours, ours, or none
  • Your logo on every clip
  • Editing in the browser before you download
  • MP4, thumbnail, title, description and hashtags
  • Suggested times to post

The rules

How minutes are counted, in full.

  1. 01

    A job counts the full length of the video you send, rounded up to the next minute. 18:05 counts as 19.

  2. 02

    Minutes are set aside when a job starts and only taken when it finishes. A job that fails, or that you cancel, costs nothing.

  3. 03

    Asking for more Shorts from the same video, up to 20, or longer ones, costs nothing extra.

  4. 04

    The free tier ends at 10 Shorts or 10 minutes of video, whichever comes first, and is once per person.

  5. 05

    Finished Shorts are kept for 30 days, then deleted. Download what you want to keep.
